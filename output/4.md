Goal

Puzzle
Can you find and circle the unique features of Hampi? Then, use the first letters of your answers to fill in the blank: What is the capital of the Vijayanagar Empire? ___

Setup
Present the page with a large illustration and the feature list below it.

Instructions
Look at the illustration of Hampi carefully.
Find and circle the seven unique features listed.
Take the first letter of each identified feature to spell a word related to Hampi's significance.

Rules
Each identified feature must be circled, and the first letters will form a single word related to Hampi.

Components
