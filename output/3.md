Hampi Hidden Wonders
Visual Perception · Classic Spot-the-Difference · easy-medium · ages 6–10 · 15 min

Review
Replace vague puzzle language with the named items, counts, and placements.
The answer "Hampi" is written in the child-facing text. The child should reach it only by playing.

Goal
Find at least 5 hidden objects that represent the unique facts about Hampi.

Puzzle
This scene shows the beautiful city of Hampi, which was the capital of the Vijayanagar Empire. It's a UNESCO World Heritage site, but some things in this picture don’t belong! Can you find at least 5 hidden objects that are out of place?

Setup
Lay out the printed page with the illustration at the center and the label at the top.

Instructions
1. Look closely at the illustration of Hampi.
2. Search for at least 5 objects that don’t belong in the historical setting.
3. Write down the names of the hidden objects in the blank area below.

Rules
All hidden objects should be identifiable and clearly out of place.
The child must find and write down at least 5 objects.
Encourage creativity if they notice additional objects that seem out of place.

Components
A large illustration of Hampi showcasing various historical buildings and landscapes.
Five out-of-place objects that represent modern items (a smartphone, a skateboard, a hot air balloon, a bicycle, and a robot).
A small label at the top saying 'Can you spot the hidden wonders?'
A blank area below the illustration for the child to write their findings.

Materials
Printed page.

Playable state
Smartphone — object — A modern device that does not belong to the historical scene.
Skateboard — object — A contemporary item that contrasts with the ancient setting.
Hot air balloon — object — An invention not present during the Vijayanagar Empire.
Bicycle — object — A modern mode of transport that would not fit into Hampi's historical context.
Robot — object — A futuristic concept that is out of place in the historical scene.

Fact connection
The child identifies hidden objects that represent modern elements in the scene.
Hampi is the capital of the Vijayanagar Empire.
The hidden objects like the smartphone and robot show the contrast with Hampi's historical context.

Differences
Smartphone - represents modern technology that didn't exist in the time of Hampi.
Skateboard - a contemporary mode of transport not seen in historical settings.
Hot air balloon - a modern invention not used during the Vijayanagar Empire.
Bicycle - a common present-day vehicle that wouldn’t be found in Hampi.
Robot - a futuristic concept that contrasts with the ancient architecture.

Final answer
Hampi

Answer
The capital of the Vijayanagar Empire is Hampi. The child discovers this by identifying hidden objects like a smartphone and a skateboard, which are modern items that do not fit the historical context.
