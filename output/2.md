Doodle Discoveries of Hampi
Word Puzzles · Acrostic Harvest · medium · ages 8–14 · 15 min

Goal
Create doodles and observations that showcase what makes Hampi special.

Puzzle
What’s so special about Hampi? Use the words below to create doodles and notes about Hampi. Then, answer the question: What empire was Hampi the capital of? _____

Setup
Print the page with the doodle area and word list visible.

Instructions
1. Look at the list of words related to Hampi.
2. Take the first letter of each word and write them down.
3. Use these letters to create a fun doodle about Hampi's significance.
4. In the blank space, write down what empire Hampi was the capital of.

Rules
Each word in the list relates to Hampi and can only be used once.
The doodle must incorporate all the letters from the word list.

Components
A printed page featuring a doodle area, a list of words related to Hampi, and a blank space for the final answer.

Materials
Printed page.

Letters
V, E, W, H, S

Final answer
Vijayanagar Empire

Answer
Hampi was the capital of the Vijayanagar Empire. The doodles and notes collected from the first letters of the words reveal Hampi's historical significance.
